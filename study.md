# Competitive study

The current card-game leaderboard is blind: it displays no ranks, scores, or paid entries yet. There are therefore no current paid-place screenshots to compare.

The published best-game leaderboard places PEONY SKY first, GLASSWRIGHT second, and BRASS LEVIATHAN third. Their descriptions each foreground a distinctive core interaction and multiple interacting systems rather than a generic arcade premise. The published spot-the-difference leaderboard places The Witch's Apothecary, Double Take, and Lantern Lane first through third; Double Take's description specifically highlights multiple scenes and keyboard play. These are different genres, so their rankings are not direct evidence of what wins a card game; their titles alone do not establish screenshot quality or file sizes.

Our target is a complete, immediately readable card duel, not a copy of those entries: visible opposing lanes and hand, colour-and-shape suits, a clear resource economy, combinations and readable enemy intent, plus a title and ending. The large cover must carry the story at contact-sheet size.
